## Steps in Suno (about five minutes per song)

1. Go to suno.com and sign in.
2. Click Create, then switch on Custom mode.
3. Paste the song's lyrics block into Lyrics, its style line into Style of Music, any exclude line into Exclude styles, and its title.
4. Click Create. Suno makes two takes per run. Listen for the one where the chorus lands hardest and the words are clearest. Run it again if neither does.
5. On the winning take, open the three-dot menu, choose Download, then MP3.
6. Save it in `D:\DJS Projects\youth-career-night\` named after the song (for example `lights-come-back-on.mp3`) and tell Claude it is there.

Claude then times each lyric line to the audio with faster-whisper, using these lyrics as the wording, and builds it into the animated video page.
